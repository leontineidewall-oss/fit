01 / how it dresses youfour rules. no stylist.

your biggest bag

the hoodie

Its ticker is printed on your chest. The colourway comes from its contract address, so every coin has its own.

your next 3 bags

coin patches

One patch per bag, colour picked by its contract. Bags worth under $1 don't count.

your dead coins

stitched on

Any coin you still hold with no market, or under $1k of liquidity, is a grey stitched patch. Up to four show.

your 24h move

the face

Value-weighted 24h change of your live bags. Down 10%: tears. Flat: shades. Up 10%: grin. Up 30%: gold chain.
